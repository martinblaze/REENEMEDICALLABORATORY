Our services

What diagnostic services does Reene Medical Diagnostics offer?
We offer clinical laboratory testing, ultrasound scanning, CT scan, MRI scan, digital X-ray imaging and ECG testing at our centre on 50 Nwaziki Avenue, Awada, Obosi, Onitsha LGA. See all services.
What laboratory tests are available?
Our laboratory covers complete blood count (CBC), blood chemistry and metabolic panels, liver and kidney function tests, lipid profile, blood sugar, thyroid function, hormone tests, urinalysis, HIV/hepatitis and STI screening, tumour markers and electrolyte studies. Full laboratory list.
What types of ultrasound scan do you offer?
Obstetric and gynaecological, abdominal, pelvic, breast, thyroid and neck, and musculoskeletal ultrasound — plus 3D and 4D pregnancy scans and Doppler studies, performed by certified sonographers. More on ultrasound.

Preparation & results

How should I prepare for my test?
It depends on the test. Some blood tests require fasting; some scans require a full bladder or other preparation. Tell us which test you are having when you book and we will give you the specific instructions beforehand.
When will I get my results?
Turnaround varies by test — routine laboratory results are typically faster than specialised investigations. We confirm the expected timeline for your specific test when you book or check in.

Safety

Is ultrasound safe during pregnancy?
Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves rather than ionising radiation, which is why it is the standard imaging method used routinely in pregnancy care worldwide. Scans should still be carried out for a clinical reason by a trained operator — discuss the timing and number of scans with your doctor or midwife.
Is a digital X-ray safe?
X-ray imaging uses a small dose of ionising radiation. Digital systems generally require a lower dose than older film-based ones and produce images faster. Tell your radiographer if you are pregnant or think you might be, so the examination can be reviewed before going ahead.
