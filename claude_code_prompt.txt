## KEY PRINCIPLE
The difference between AI-generated and professional is: SPECIFICITY + RESTRAINT + POLISH

- Specificity: Real numbers, real photos, real copy
- Restraint: White space, selective colors, simple interactions  
- Polish: Proper spacing, smooth animations, professional typography

Don't try to make it "wow" - make it "trustworthy". A medical site should feel professional and clean, not exciting and AI-designed.

START WITH SPACING & TYPOGRAPHY - those two alone will remove the AI feel by 60%.
