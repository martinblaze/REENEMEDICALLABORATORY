Who we are

A technology-driven diagnostic centre built for reliable, affordable and timely results — serving Onitsha and the communities around it.

We combine modern diagnostic technology with skilled professionals, offering routine tests and preventive screening designed around patient convenience, clarity and confidence.

Our story
What we do

Six diagnostic disciplines under one roof.

01 Laboratory Tests Blood chemistry, haematology, hormones, urinalysis and screening — processed under strict quality control. 02 Ultrasound Scan Obstetric, abdominal, pelvic and Doppler studies — including 3D and 4D pregnancy scans by certified sonographers. 03 CT Scan Cross-sectional imaging for tumours, internal injury, clots and structural abnormalities — contrast and non-contrast. 04 MRI Scan Detailed soft-tissue, neurological, spinal and musculoskeletal imaging with no radiation exposure. 05 Digital X-Ray Chest, skeletal, spine and extremity radiography — clearer images, reduced radiation, immediate results. 06 ECG Resting ECG, stress testing and 24-hour Holter monitoring to detect arrhythmia and cardiac disease early.
