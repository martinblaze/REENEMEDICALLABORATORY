About us

Diagnostics with intention.

Our story

Reene Medical Diagnostics is a technology-driven diagnostic healthcare provider based in Awada, Obosi, Onitsha LGA, Anambra State, serving Onitsha and surrounding communities.

We deliver reliable, affordable and timely diagnostic services that support better clinical decisions and improved health outcomes.

Combining modern diagnostic technology with skilled professionals, we offer routine tests and preventive screening packages designed around patient convenience, clarity and confidence.

Beyond a conventional diagnostic centre.

Reene is building a scalable healthcare platform through digital workflows, structured reporting and efficient service delivery. We are committed to expanding access to quality diagnostics, strengthening preventive healthcare and improving lives across South-East Nigeria.

Over the years we have earned a reputation for professionalism, reliability and compassionate care. Our patients trust us not just because of our equipment, but because of the people behind it.

Our mission

To provide accurate, affordable, and timely diagnostic services by integrating digital systems, efficient workflows, and preventive health solutions, while scaling impact through partnerships, innovation, and operational excellence with the highest standard of patient care and professionalism.

To build a scalable, technology-driven diagnostic healthcare platform that transforms access to reliable medical testing, while becoming a trusted and preferred provider of high-quality diagnostic services in Anambra State and across South-East Nigeria, recognized for excellence in service delivery and patient satisfaction.
