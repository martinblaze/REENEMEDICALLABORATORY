Who We Are

Redefining Access to Quality Healthcare

Reene Medical Diagnostics is a technology-driven diagnostic healthcare provider based in Awada, Obosi, Idemili North LGA, Anambra State, serving Onitsha and surrounding communities.

We deliver reliable, affordable, and timely diagnostic services that support better clinical decisions and improved health outcomes. Combining modern diagnostic technology with skilled professionals, we offer routine tests and preventive screening packages designed around patient convenience, clarity, and confidence.

Beyond being a conventional diagnostic centre, Reene is building a scalable healthcare platform through digital workflows, structured reporting, and efficient service delivery. We are committed to expanding access to quality diagnostics, strengthening preventive healthcare, and improving lives across South-East Nigeria.
