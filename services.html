  • Abdominal
  • Pelvic
  • Breast
  • Thyroid & neck

  • 3D / 4D pregnancy scans

03 — Imaging

CT Scan

Detailed cross-sectional images of the body, allowing precise diagnosis of a wide range of conditions using advanced CT technology.

  • Brain & head
  • Chest
  • Abdominal & pelvic
  • Spine
  • CT angiography
  • CT-guided procedures
  • Contrast & non-contrast

Used to detect tumours, internal injuries, blood clots, infections and structural abnormalities.

Full guide to CT scanning
04 — Imaging

MRI Scan

Detailed images of organs, soft tissues, bones and internal structures without using radiation — superior diagnostic capability, non-invasive.

  • Brain & neurological
  • Spinal
  • Musculoskeletal
  • Abdominal
  • Pelvic
  • Cardiac
  • MR angiography

No radiation exposure, excellent soft-tissue contrast and detailed visualisation.

Full guide to MRI scanning
05 — Imaging

Digital X-Ray

Digital X-ray imaging providing high-quality images with reduced radiation exposure, ensuring faster processing and accurate diagnosis.

  • Chest (PA & lateral)
  • Abdominal
  • Skeletal
  • Spine
  • Extremity
  • Skull
  • Pelvic

Clearer images, minimal radiation, immediate results and electronic storage.

Full guide to digital X-ray
06 — Cardiac

ECG

Electrocardiogram testing to assess the heart's electrical activity and detect cardiac conditions — quick, painless and non-invasive.

  • Resting ECG
  • Exercise stress test
  • 24-hour Holter monitoring
  • Heart rate & rhythm analysis

Detects arrhythmias, coronary artery disease, heart enlargement and other cardiac conditions.
